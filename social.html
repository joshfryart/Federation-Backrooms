Every run's crew gets its own social life (js/social.js, docs/social.md): mess tables, department cells, route crews, hobby groups, cliques, couples, mentors, rivals, feuds, gossip, a favour book, the one everyone likes and the one nobody trusts. Ties come from who works, eats and sleeps near whom (department, watch, duty rooms, deck, rank) plus seeded chemistry. Matrix: rows/columns sorted by department then watch; green = positive tie (brighter = stronger), red = negative, gold = partner. Click a cell or a name.
