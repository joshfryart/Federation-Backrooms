Starfarers: The Backdecks

Stardate unknown · Something is aboard

You are the last crew member awake aboard a United Starfarers' Fleet vessel that keeps changing. The corridors repeat. The hum never stops. The lights go out sometimes. Something is still walking. Something is hunting you — a Gronk, a drone, a hologram with a grin, or worse. Find the logic chips, power the tubelift, and descend — ship after ship, deck after deck.
